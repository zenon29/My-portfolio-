    About

    Learning in public, one project at a time

    I'm an engineering student working through the fundamentals: how programs are structured, how the web fits together, and how to think about problems before writing code for them. Most of what I know so far came from building something small, breaking it, and figuring out why.

    Software development and AI are what pull me in. I like that both reward patience — you read, you try, you get it wrong, and eventually the idea clicks and stays. Right now that means Python and JavaScript day to day, with data structures and algorithms as the slower, harder track running alongside.

    Long term I want to build software people actually use, and work on problems that are difficult enough to be worth the years it takes to get good at them. Short term: ship the next project, and make it better than the last one.

    Away from the screen I'm usually chasing whatever has my attention that month — which is roughly how I pick my projects too.
